# ImHex-ColoredBytes-Plugin

colors bytes based on https://simonomi.dev/blog/color-code-your-bytes/

https://github.com/simonomi/simonomi.github.io/blob/aa55ea855c17d5253d85d0440f41871aadc27b83/_includes/code.css#L51-L68

building (on linux, "works my machine")

```sh
IMHEX_SDK_PATH=/usr/share/imhex/sdk/ cmake .. -GNinja
ninja
cp coloredbytes.hexplug ~/.local/share/imhex/plugins
```
